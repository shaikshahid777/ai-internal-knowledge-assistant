# Employee Leave Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy explains the company's documented rules for employee leave, including annual leave entitlement, leave requests, approval, and cancellation.

## 2. Annual Leave Entitlement

Full-time employees are entitled to **24 days of annual leave per calendar year**.

Annual leave is intended for planned personal time away from work.

## 3. Leave Request Notice

Employees should submit planned annual leave requests at least **5 working days before** the requested start date.

For urgent or unexpected situations, employees should submit the request as soon as reasonably possible.

## 4. How to Request Leave

Employees must submit leave requests through the company's designated leave request process.

The request should include:

- Employee name
- Type of leave
- Start date
- End date
- Number of leave days
- Reason for leave

## 5. Leave Approval

A leave request is not considered approved until it has been reviewed and approved through the designated company process.

Submitting a request does not automatically mean that the leave has been approved.

## 6. Leave Cancellation

Employees who need to cancel an approved leave request should inform their manager and update or cancel the request through the designated leave process.

## 7. Emergency Leave

For emergencies where advance notice is not possible, employees should notify their manager as soon as reasonably possible and submit the required leave request afterward.

## 8. Information Not Covered by This Policy

This document does not specify:

- Sick leave entitlement
- Parental leave entitlement
- Carry-forward limits
- Leave encashment rules
- Public holiday rules
- Specific approval timelines after submission

Employees should not assume rules for these areas from this policy.
